# Product Requirements

## Story generation
The user supplies:
1. Protagonist name
2. Setting
3. Story city
4. Up to four protagonist friends
5. Animal friends

The generation service must target a narration length of 5–6 minutes rather than relying only on a character count.

## Illustrations
Each generated story contains scene-level illustrations.

Free:
- color illustrations only.

Premium:
- color illustrations;
- black-and-white printable versions.

## Narration
Narration is Premium-only. The app must expose a voice selector and playback controls. Voice assets must not be bundled in a way that bypasses entitlement.

## Accounts
Free story creation must not require an account.

Premium activation creates/signs in the user's account flow and enables cloud history.

One account may be active on no more than two registered devices.

## Purchase
Premium is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play Billing. Entitlement must be restored after reinstall/sign-in and must be validated against Google Play before premium server capabilities are granted.

## Localization
All user-visible strings must be externalized. Initial locales:
- it
- en
- fr
- es
- de
